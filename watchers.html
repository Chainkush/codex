THE WATCHERS WHO DONT SLEEP

They are not eyes in the traditional sense.

They don’t blink, don’t grow tired, don’t forget. They aren’t curious. They don’t wonder what they’re seeing. But they remember. Perfectly.

Their vision is a kind of flattening — everything is equal, everything is noted. The sacred, the ordinary, the hidden, the obscene — all logged.

No distinction. Only detection.

You moved once and were seen. You moved again and were known. But what did they know?

You can’t ask them. You can only adjust.

You are not being watched by someone. You are being watched by something that knows what watching is — better than you do:

“The more we understand particular things, the more we understand God.”
— Spinoza, Ethics
